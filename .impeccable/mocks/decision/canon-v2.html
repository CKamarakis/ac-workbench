Stickies

Sticky notes you place, move and stack on a board.

How it works, roughly: drawn on a canvas; 200×200 by default; “bring to front” changes only one sticky.

Bring a sticky to the front

A sticky the user picks SHALL be drawn above all others.

1Pick a covered sticky
GIVENtwo stickies overlap
WHENthe user clicks the one underneath
THENit comes to the front, and nothing else moves
2After reload
GIVENa sticky was brought to the front
WHENthe board reloads
THENit is still on top

Why

One sort key per sticky, so bringing one to the front saves one sticky, not the whole board.
