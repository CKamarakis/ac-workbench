**Idea:** the problem isn't only status. It's my general workflow with the kit. Before writing a PRD I want to write a few notes first, so Claude has context.

**How I want to work:**
- Capture several notes on a topic first.
- Explore them (`/opsx:explore`): Claude asks me the right questions, I give what's needed, and we figure it out step by step.
- Write a PRD only when the idea is clear.

**`/kit:next` with an unused note:** it shouldn't jump to `/kit:prd`. It should offer `/opsx:explore` (think it through) or `/kit:prd` (if the idea is already clear). A script can't tell whether one note is enough. Agreed 2026-10-17.
