## How it works, roughly
A script rebuilds one offline page from the specs, archived changes, decision records and these notes, at no token cost. `/kit:docs` only drafts what the specs don't hold, and writes it on your yes.

## Why

- **Living docs are optional**: small or one-day projects don't need a page; nothing docs-related happens until `/kit:docs` creates `knowledge/docs/`.
- **Draft notes on a yes**: the why is your reasoning; the agent only proposes it and never invents a reason the sources don't give.
- **Open decisions on the page**: a decision put off stays visible, even after its change is archived, so Later doesn't quietly become never.
